### Tell us something you're interested in, then.

I suppose I can tell you a little bit about the game I've been enjoying lately, Death Stranding 2: On the Beach.

For those who don't know, Death Stranding is a Kojima video game set in a post-apocalyptic world in which decay in all its forms consumes the world, but instead of being some legendary hero, you're just a guy trying to make deliveries. When I played the original Death Stranding in 2020, I had recently built my new gaming PC specifically to have the specifications to be able to run Death Stranding. I had built a Death Stranding machine.

When I was playing the original, I was pretty damn depressed, and the game was one of the few things I had that I could return to and work on. I was unemployed. It was the middle of COVID lockdowns. It both reflected the world I saw and also was an escape from it.
